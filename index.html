How it works

Four steps from link to file.

  1. 1

    Connect your account

    Sign in through Instagram's official authorization window. This gives InstaSave read-only access to your own media. Using only the upload tool? Skip this step.

  2. 2

    Paste the link

    Copy the link of one of your Reels, videos, photos or carousels and paste it into the box.

  3. 3

    Preview

    InstaSave finds the post in your account and shows a preview with the available formats.

  4. 4

    Download

    Download the original file, or each slide of a carousel. You can also upload a file and convert it to another format.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any public Instagram post?

No. Instagram's official API only lets an app read media from the account that authorized it. InstaSave therefore works for your own posts (and accounts you are authorized to manage). It does not bypass privacy settings and cannot fetch other people's posts.

Do I need to log in?

For link downloads you authorize InstaSave once through Instagram's own login window. We never receive your password. The upload tool needs no login.

Which Instagram accounts are supported?

The official Instagram API supports professional accounts (Business or Creator). Personal accounts can switch to a professional account for free in the Instagram app settings. You can always use the upload tool with files saved on your device.

Why can't I find an older post?

InstaSave searches the most recent posts of the connected account. Very old posts can fall outside that range, and Instagram may withhold the file for posts containing copyrighted audio.

What formats are supported?

Instagram media is delivered in its original format (JPG for photos, MP4 for videos). With the upload tool you can convert images to JPG, PNG or WebP, and videos to MP4, WebM or MP3 audio, when the server supports it.

Are stories and private accounts supported?

No. Stories, highlights and content you are not authorized to access are not supported.

Does InstaSave keep my files?

Instagram downloads are streamed and not stored. Uploaded and converted files are deleted automatically after a short time, and you can delete them yourself at any moment. See the Privacy Policy.

Is it okay to re-use downloaded content?

Only download and share media you own or have permission to use. Respect copyright and the rights of the people shown in the content.

About Us
