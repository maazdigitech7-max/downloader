Privacy Policy

Template for the operator of this site. Review it, adapt it to your situation and have it checked by a qualified professional before going live.

What we process

  • Instagram authorization (optional). If you connect Instagram, we receive a short-lived access token with read-only permission for your own media (instagram_business_basic), your Instagram user ID and your username. We never receive your password.
  • Links you paste. Used only to find the matching post in the account you connected.
  • Images you convert. Processed in memory only to produce the converted file, then discarded. They are never written to disk.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used in memory for rate limiting and security. A strictly necessary session cookie keeps you connected.

How long we keep it

  • Your Instagram access token is stored only inside an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie in your browser. The server cannot be asked to show it to scripts, it is not kept on our servers, and it expires after about one hour or when you disconnect.
  • Instagram media is streamed to you and not stored on our server.
  • Converted images are returned immediately and not retained.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't use advertising trackers and we don't build profiles. We don't access content you haven't authorized.

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary, HttpOnly cookies for your session and for protecting the sign-in flow.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time with the Disconnect button, and you can revoke InstaSave's access in your Instagram account settings under "Apps and websites".
